HOW TO USE GINKO

From first launch
to private space.

A quick guide for the parts of Ginko that matter most.

01

ADD MUSIC FOLDERS

Choose the folders containing your music. Ginko scans them locally and builds the Library.

02

SEARCH & PLAY

Search across titles, artists, albums and folders, then play directly from the results.

03

BUILD PLAYLISTS

Create playlists from songs in different folders. Embedded cover art is used when available.

04

OPEN PRIVATE SPACE

Tap the center artwork 7 times within the short entry window to reveal the private-space route.

05

USE YOUR PIN

PIN 1 opens Space A, PIN 2 opens Space B and PIN 3 is reserved for emergency wipe.

06

LOCK AGAIN

Lock manually or use Auto-lock from Settings so private content does not stay open indefinitely.

THE EMERGENCY PATH

One PIN.
One irreversible action.

The emergency PIN is intentionally separate from the two access PINs. Triggering it wipes both private spaces and returns the vault to its setup state.

QUESTIONS

Straight answers.

What is Ginko Player?

A local-first Android music player with a separate encrypted private-space feature.

Does Ginko upload my music?

Ginko is designed around local music stored on your device. It is not a cloud music service.

How do I open Private Space?

From the normal player, tap the center artwork 7 times within the short entry window to reveal the private-space route.

What do the three PINs do?

PIN 1 opens Private Space A, PIN 2 opens Private Space B and PIN 3 triggers emergency wipe. The three PINs are separate.

Can I use Ginko only as a music player?

Yes. The normal music player works independently from the private-space feature.

Does removing a music folder delete it?

No. Removing a folder from Ginko only removes it from the player's library configuration.

Where can I report bugs?

Use the GitHub repository's Issues page after opening the source link.
