LEGAL / PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

Ginko Player is designed as a local-first Android music player. The app scans music folders you select on your device so it can build your local library.

Local music

Your selected music folders are used to discover and play local audio. The website does not upload your music files.

Private Space

Files placed in Private Space are stored in app-private storage and encrypted by the app's vault system. Temporary decrypted cache files may be created when a protected file needs to be viewed or played.
