Privacy Policy

Overview

Ginko Player is designed as a local Android music player with private encrypted storage.

Music and files

The app may request access to audio files or a folder selected by you so it can build a local library. Files placed into private spaces are stored in app-private storage and encrypted by the application.

Private spaces

Private-space access uses PIN-based key wrapping and Android Keystore-backed key material. The private vault is designed around local storage rather than a cloud service.

Website and advertising

This website may use advertising or analytics in the future. If third-party services are enabled, this policy should be updated with the providers and information they process before those services are used.
