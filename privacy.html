Privacy
Policy.

This page describes the privacy approach of the Ginko Player Android application. Update the wording here if the production app's data practices change.

Local music

Ginko is designed around music stored locally on your Android device and selected through Android's folder access mechanisms.

Private Space

Private Space uses app-private encrypted storage. Access is protected by the application's PIN system.

Network use

Ginko is not designed as a cloud music library. This website is separate from the Android app and may use normal web hosting infrastructure.

Contact

For questions or privacy requests, use the creator links on the Ginko Player website or Contact through our email: forenseal@gmail.com.
