Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Overview

Ginko Player is an Android application focused on local music playback and a separate private space for protected files. This policy describes the general way the app handles information.

2. Music library

Ginko Player can access music folders that you select on your device so it can find, organize and play your local audio files. The music library is intended to operate on your device and does not require you to create an online account.

3. Private Space

Files placed in Ginko's private space are stored in app-private storage and protected by the app's vault system. Private-space files are kept separate from the normal music library. The exact encryption and storage implementation may change between app versions as the project develops.

4. PINs and access

The app uses PIN-based access for its private-space features. Do not share your PINs with other people. If you forget a PIN, recovery options depend on the specific app version.

5. Permissions

Depending on the Android version and the features you use, Ginko may request permission to access audio files or selected folders. These permissions are used to provide the features you choose to use.

6. Notifications and playback

When background playback is active, Android may display playback controls through the system notification or media-control interface. This is part of normal Android media playback behavior.

7. Website

This website is primarily a project and download site. It does not require a Ginko account. The website may contain links to GitHub, VirusTotal, Buy Me a Coffee and other third-party services. Those services have their own privacy policies and terms.

8. Third-party services

Ginko Player itself is not presented as a streaming service. If you choose to follow a third-party link, such as a GitHub release or VirusTotal scan, your interaction with that service is governed by the service provider's own policies.

9. Data collection

Ginko Player is designed around local use. The project does not intentionally require personal information such as your phone number or email address simply to use the music player. Future versions may introduce features that require updates to this policy.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app changes. The latest version will be published on this page with a new update date.

11. Contact

For support, bug reports and project discussions, use the Ginko Player GitHub repository and its Issues section.
